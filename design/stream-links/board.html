Stream links: where a link opens

Charles, 22 Nov: whatever a stream creates (files, URLs, side streams) is a link. A click opens a side panel, and a right click offers "open in app" or "open in browser". The browser is a real in-app one: WebView2 on Windows and WKWebView on macOS through wry. Linux has no GTK loop under GPUI, so the page box says so there.
