Where does it work?

Shared with chamy's Claude Code: both edit it. Pick its own copy to keep their changes apart.

01 · PalettePaper, not neon

Warm off-white ground, white cards and near-black ink. Pastels are reserved for people and status — never for glows or gradients behind the UI.

02 · TypeFewer sizes, bigger type

Geist with a 30/15/12 rhythm and sentence case. Mono only for counts, branches and shortcuts. The all-caps micro-label layer is gone.

03 · ShapeSoft and continuous

16–20px cards, 12px controls, full pills. Borders are hairlines or disappear into a surface shift. No dashed boxes, glow rings or hard dividers.

04 · IntegrationOne frame, not a dashboard

Rail and sidebar share a surface. The top bar is almost empty. The page owns its scroll and its one primary action. Accounts, settings and update live at the rail bottom.
