The nine agent colours

Face moods

The eleven orbling moods. Only the face changes. Working turns the head into a cog, typing turns it into three small heads, and the question mark, exclamation mark and z's show at 28px and up. Watching follows your pointer. Reduced motion shows still faces.

How it is built in the app

  • All vector, no image files: two circles, the face and the cog are plain path fills in GPUI. This replaces the generated textured spheres, so the seeded-texture code is no longer needed.
  • Face and cog: eye positions per mood and time, and the cog's angle, are pure functions in cauldron-chrome with tests, driven by the motion clock we already have.
  • Colour: the agent's dot, from the existing token list.
  • Cost: smaller than the textured version. The mood from core is limited to what it knows today, see the table below.
