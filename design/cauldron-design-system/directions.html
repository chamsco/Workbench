Decision: A, tone steps

Load and scanning. A list of 30 threads in B carries 30 coloured pills; in A only the rows that need Charles carry colour, so they are found in one glance. That is the job of the sidebar.

Consistency. A keeps the approved radii (6/4/3), the single ember accent and Segoe UI Variable. B would reopen three decisions that were settled.

Build cost in GPUI. A is flat fills. B puts a ring and a shadow on every surface, which GPUI draws per element and which the wireframes already cut once.

Self-critique of A. Near-black with one vermilion accent is itself a common AI look. A only escapes it if the accent stays rare (focus, one primary per view, needs-you) and the neutrals carry the hierarchy. Tone steps in light mode are subtle (white card on #f3f2f4), so hover and selection must use a real fill, not a hairline.
