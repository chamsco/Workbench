Split panes, two directions

This is how the thread card could split, so you can see the idea before it is built. Both directions narrow the gap between panes to 4px, let you drag the line between two panes, and allow up to four panes. Today the app opens one fixed 292px half with an 8px gap. Try both mocks below: drag a gutter, add a pane, close one.
