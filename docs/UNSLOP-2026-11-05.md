# UNSLOP pass — framed sheet, flat surfaces

The direction comes from `design-taste-frontend` / tasteskill v2, app-screen run:
**Framed sheet** (seed 116668374), for a developer workspace, keeping the warm
palette and Inter but taking the shell, spacing and surfaces from the sheet.

The two problems in the before screenshots:

1. **Three floating systems on one screen.** The project rail, the top command
   pill and the bottom utility pill all floated over a flat sidebar and page.
   Each had its own radius, shadow, tint and spacing, so the chrome read as
   mixed parts rather than one frame.
2. **Ornament instead of hierarchy.** A gradient hero, six separate stat cards
   with tinted icon squares, 7–9px uppercase mono labels, violet/peach chrome
   accents and shadows on everything. The Today question ("what happened on
   this day?") was set behind a date billboard.

## Direction (unslop)

- **Product:** a local AI-agent workspace for one person or a small team; projects, sessions, machines and bots.
- **Character:** calm, exact, a little warm.
- **World:** a paper desk and a terminal; ink, brass and soft paper, not neon.
- **Moments:** Today's activity timeline and stat rail, the Code transcript, the bot faces.
- **Signature:** the three-rail chrome — four modes in the header, attention/work at the top of the sidebar, places and utilities in the footer — plus one live status dot.

## What changed

- **One framed shell.** Rail, sidebar and 48px top bar share one tinted ground.
  The page is a white sheet inset 8px from the right and bottom, 1px edge,
  12px radius, no shadow. The project rail is a flush 48px column. The sidebar
  is 232px.
- **Top command row.** One flat row, centered: four modes, five work links,
  three places (Today, Machines, Team), separated by 1px rules. Active is a
  2px ink underline, not an inverted pill. No background, border or blur.
- **Bottom rail.** The status strip is a 32px flush strip. Machine on the left;
  Settings, Light/Night, Glass, Help and account on the right. No floating
  pill, no duplicate search/help in the top bar.
- **Three rails.** Four mode icons sit in the header; the five attention/work
  icons sit at the top of the sidebar; Today, Machines and Team sit in the
  footer with utilities. The chat's token/cost usage is centered under the
  composer, not in the footer.
- **No duplicate controls.** Theme buttons left the sidebar profile; settings,
  refresh and account left the project rail; Today/help/machine left the top
  bar. Each action now has one home.
- **Flat surfaces.** Cards and panels use a 1px border and a 10px radius. No
  rest shadows, no hover lift. Radii are 10 for panels, 6 for controls, 999
  only for avatars and dots.
- **Typography.** Eyebrows removed; mono is kept for file paths and version
  strings only. Labels are sentence case at 11–12px. The Today h1 is the date
  at 29px/600; the hero card is gone.
- **One data panel.** Today's six stat cards became one bordered rail with
  divided sections. The decisions donut is an SVG ring, not a CSS gradient.
  The Home/Limits KPI row is one divided strip, not three tiles.
- **Icon chips removed.** Machine, row, setting and Getting-started icons are
  plain marks, not icons in tinted squares.
- **Grids close.** Machines are three columns, five hosts plus one Pair tile.
  Team is three columns, four bots plus a two-column New bot tile.
- **Glass is one skin.** The shared scene and sheet tint remain, but cards no
  longer blur individually. Glass is optional; light/dark use the flat sheet.
